2017

ABS-CBN Entertainment feature connected to Miss Q&A

ABS-CBN Entertainment published coverage of Francine's participation in the Miss Q&A segment of It's Showtime. Earlier coverage credited her as Francis Ebenguan Bautista, while she now publicly and professionally uses the name Francine Marie Bautista.

Source record: ABS-CBN Entertainment article titled “Francis Ebenguan Bautista, sinubukang agawin ang korona mula kay reigning Miss Q & A Juliana Parizcova Segovia.”
