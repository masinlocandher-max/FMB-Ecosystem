Factual profile

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Filipina creative director, brand strategist, public relations practitioner, entrepreneur, photographer and storyteller whose work connects branding, communications, tourism, culture, education, technology and community impact.

Background and public work

Bautista is a Tourism Management graduate whose professional experience spans teaching, training, communications, branding, photography and digital product development. Her public record includes appearances in ABS-CBN's It's Showtime through the Miss Q&A segment, as well as later pageant participation and independent pageant-media coverage.

She is the founder of FMB&CO., an umbrella ecosystem for independently positioned creative, cultural, educational and digital initiatives. Projects associated with the ecosystem include SENZ, With Love, FMB, Yoni, The Cognita Institute, Mabayani and PageantIndex.

Leadership philosophy

Her work is guided by four stated core values: kindness, honesty, understanding and service to humanity. These values inform how she approaches public communication, brand building, education, cultural preservation and community-oriented projects.
