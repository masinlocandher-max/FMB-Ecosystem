Data Rights

Your information should not become a mystery.

Yoni keeps some records only in your browser and handles existing-member authentication and profile data remotely. The controls are different for each.

1. Local records you control directly

  • Yoni stores check-ins, journal entries, chat history, response-mode preference, reminder preference, theme cache, local profile cache, locally logged unrecognized questions, and any older Kind Wall preview queue in browser local storage.
  • Use the Privacy and personal control screen to export a JSON copy of the current local records.
  • Delete check-in history or journal entries from that screen.
  • Use “Delete all local Yoni data” to remove Yoni's current local-storage records from that browser.
  • Signing out of the remote account does not by itself erase those local records.

2. Remote account and profile information

Existing-member sign-in uses Supabase Authentication. Yoni also reads an existing Supabase profile record and currently uses the member name or display name when present, username, biography, avatar preset, and app theme. The current interface can update username, biography, avatar preset, and app theme. Full name is shown as read-only in the current Yoni profile interface.

3. Requests you may send

For available remote authentication or profile information, you may ask about access, correction, deletion, or account closure, subject to identity verification and applicable technical, security, provider, and legal limitations. Local browser records are generally controlled on the device itself and are not available to the administrator through a Yoni journal or chat database.

4. How to submit a remote-data request

Email withlovefmb@gmail.com with the subject “Data Rights Request.” Include:

  • The verified email connected to the existing account.
  • The type of request.
  • The specific remote account or profile information involved.
  • Enough detail to understand and locate the account.

Do not email a password, one-time code, government identification number, financial credential, or full medical record.

5. Identity verification

Before releasing, changing, or deleting remote account information, reasonable steps may be taken to confirm that the requester controls the verified email or account. Additional verification should be limited to what is reasonably needed for the request and risk involved.

6. Local export versus remote access request

Yoni's in-app export is generated directly from the browser and can include the local check-ins, journals, chat, local profile cache, older Kind Wall preview queue, theme, reminder preference, response mode, and locally logged unrecognized questions stored there. A remote account-data request is different: it concerns information actually held by the authentication/profile service, not the local journal, chat, or check-in records that Yoni does not upload.

7. Correction

Supported profile fields such as username, biography, fruit avatar preset, and theme can be changed while signed in. Full name is currently read-only in the Yoni interface. For a remote account/profile field that cannot be corrected directly, identify the field and requested correction in the privacy email.

8. Deletion and account closure

Deleting or closing a remote authentication account is separate from deleting Yoni's local browser records. Because the journal, check-ins, chat, preferences, local profile cache, and older Kind Wall preview queue are stored locally, a remote account closure should not be assumed to erase those records from a device. Use Yoni's local delete controls or clear the relevant browser storage when local deletion is wanted.

9. Kind Wall preview data

Public Kind Wall posting is currently disabled. There is no active remote moderation queue or database of pending/approved Yoni community posts. Any older Kind Wall preview queue created by the previous browser-only behavior remains local to that browser and is included in Yoni's local export/delete-all controls.

10. Response and follow-up

A remote account/profile request will be reviewed within a reasonable period. Complex, unclear, repeated, or legally sensitive requests may require clarification or additional time. If a request cannot be completed as asked, the response should explain the reason when appropriate and lawful.

11. Complaint or unresolved concern

Start by emailing the privacy contact so the issue can be investigated. You may also seek guidance from the appropriate data-protection authority or a qualified privacy professional when a concern remains unresolved.

12. Emergency support

Data-rights email, local journals, Yoni chat, check-ins, and the Kind Wall preview are not emergency channels. When there is immediate danger, use Yoni's Human Help screen or contact appropriate emergency services.
