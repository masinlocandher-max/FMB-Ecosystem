Supporting biography

Background, pageantry and a life shaped by service

Francine Marie Bautista's work grew from Masinloc, tourism education, teaching, training, pageantry and the belief that visibility becomes more meaningful when it creates clarity, dignity and practical opportunity.

Roots and education

From Masinloc to tourism and public-facing work

Bautista is from Masinloc, Zambales. The town's culture, language, tourism potential and community life later became recurring themes in her creative, cultural and advocacy projects.

She completed a Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management at STI College Fairview. Her tourism background contributed to her interest in place branding, public experience, events, hospitality, culture and the way communities present themselves to the wider world.

Teaching and training

Professional experience built around helping people perform better

From 2019 to 2021, Bautista worked as an instructor at Northern Zambales College Inc. She later served as a BPO trainer supporting Optum and UnitedHealthcare operations from 2021 to 2022.

In 2024, she delivered call-center training for a local-government workforce initiative. These roles strengthened her experience in instruction, communication, coaching, curriculum delivery, performance support and translating complex information into practical steps.

Her later work in entrepreneurship, communications and education draws from the same foundation: understand people clearly, then build systems that help them move forward.

Pageantry and media

Pageantry became a public classroom

Bautista appeared on national television through the Miss Q&A segment of ABS-CBN's It's Showtime and later continued participating in pageants and related public events.

The experience gave her practical exposure to public speaking, presentation, pressure, image, audience perception, confidence and reinvention. Those lessons later informed her work in personal branding, pageant communications, photography, storytelling and public relations.

Her pageant background is presented as one part of a broader professional journey, not as the sole basis of her public identity.

Entrepreneurship and creative direction

Turning connected skills into ventures

Bautista now develops founder-led work across strategic communications, public relations, branding, digital products, education, photography, culture, music and pageantry.

Her ventures and initiatives include FMB&CO., SENZ Strategic Communications and Digital Solutions, The Cognita Institute, PageantIndex Philippines, With Love, FMB, Yoni and Mabayani. Each has a separate role and is presented according to its actual stage of development.

The common thread is the effort to help people, organizations, communities and ideas become clearer, more visible, better understood and more capable of creating lasting value.

Advocacy

From representation to direct community work

Her advocacy interests include women, LGBTQIA+ people, mental health, education, cultural preservation, Sambal Tina language preservation and community development.

These interests are expressed through public information, digital tools, cultural research, creative work and direct community activities. The photographs on this page document participation in volunteer and service-oriented work without inventing event names, dates or beneficiary claims.
