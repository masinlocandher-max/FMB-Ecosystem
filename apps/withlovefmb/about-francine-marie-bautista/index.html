Professional profile

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Filipina creative director, brand strategist, public relations practitioner, entrepreneur, photographer and storyteller. Her work connects branding, communications, culture, education, tourism, digital products and public perception.

Background

Work shaped by strategy, storytelling and service.

Bautista is a Tourism Management graduate whose professional experience includes teaching, training, strategic communications, branding, photography and digital product development.

Her public media record includes appearances in ABS-CBN's It's Showtime through the Miss Q&A segment, as well as later pageant participation and independent pageant-media coverage.

She leads FMB&CO., the company and creative ecosystem connected to initiatives in communications, education, culture, wellbeing and technology.
