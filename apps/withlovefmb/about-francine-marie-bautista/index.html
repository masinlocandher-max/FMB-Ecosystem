Official professional profile

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Filipina creative director, brand strategist, public relations practitioner, entrepreneur, photographer, pageant alumna and storyteller from Masinloc, Zambales. Her work connects branding, communications, culture, education, tourism, advocacy, digital products and public perception.

Background and education

Work shaped by history, strategy, storytelling and service.

Bautista was raised in Masinloc, Zambales and studied at San Andres School of Masinloc. She is pursuing a master’s degree in History at the University of the Philippines Baguio, with expected completion in 2028.

Her professional experience includes teaching, training, strategic communications, branding, photography and digital product development.

Her public media record includes appearances in ABS-CBN's It's Showtime through the Miss Q&A segment, as well as later pageant participation and independent pageant-media coverage.

She leads FMB&CO., the company and creative ecosystem connected to initiatives in communications, education, culture, wellbeing and technology.

Advocacy and core values

The principles behind the work.

Her advocacy spans women, LGBTQIA+ people, mental health, education, cultural preservation, Sambal Tina language preservation and community development.

Her stated core values are kindness, honesty, understanding and service to humanity. These principles guide how she approaches leadership, communication, community initiatives and the responsibility of creating work that affects other people.
