Official founder and professional profile

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Filipina entrepreneur, founder, creative director, brand strategist, public relations practitioner, educator, trainer, photographer and storyteller from Masinloc, Zambales. She develops ventures and initiatives across strategic communications, digital products, education, culture, pageantry and community work.
