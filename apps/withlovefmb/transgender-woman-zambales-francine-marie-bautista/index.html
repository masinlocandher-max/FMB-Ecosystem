Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista, also known as FMB, is a Masinloqueña transgender woman from Masinloc, Zambales. Her work includes entrepreneurship, communications, digital projects, education, pageantry, history, culture, philanthropy and community service.

Zambaleña identity

Rooted in Masinloc, Zambales

Masinloc is not simply a location attached to Francine's professional profile. It is the community, cultural environment and local identity that shaped much of her interest in tourism, history, language, public service and cultural preservation.

She identifies as Masinloqueña and Zambaleña and maintains projects connected to Masinloc, Sambal history, Sambal Tina language preservation, local memory and community development.

Public work

A transgender woman working across several fields

Francine's public record includes entrepreneurship, brand and marketing strategy, public relations, strategic communications, digital solutions, education and training, photography, storytelling, pageantry, tourism, philanthropy and community initiatives.

She is the founder of FMB&CO., SENZ Strategic Communications and Digital Solutions, The Cognita Institute and PageantIndex Philippines. Her cultural and public-interest projects include With Love, FMB, Yoni and Mabayani.

Her pageant and media experience includes participation in the Miss Q&A segment of It's Showtime and local pageantry. Her work also includes historical and cultural research related to Masinloc, Sambal identity and Sambal Tina language preservation.

Community service

Francine's community work is not based on holding elected or appointed political office. It is expressed through volunteer activities, educational projects, advocacy, cultural work and the development of practical information and digital resources.
