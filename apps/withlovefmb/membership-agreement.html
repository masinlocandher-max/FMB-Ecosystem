Membership Agreement

A clear promise between our space and its members.

This agreement describes the current existing-member experience. New registration remains closed.

1. The service

With love, FMB offers public reading and support information. Yoni provides existing members with a digital companion, a remote authentication/profile layer, and local browser tools such as check-ins, journals, chat history, preferences, export, and deletion controls. The Kind Wall is currently a preview only and public community posting is disabled.

2. Eligibility and accurate information

Registration is currently closed. Existing members should use the email address connected to their account and should not impersonate another person, share access credentials, or use an account to misrepresent identity.

3. Account security

  • Keep the password private and use a strong, unique password.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access promptly.
  • Do not attempt to access another member’s account or administrative tools.

4. Kind Wall status

The Yoni Kind Wall is not accepting live community submissions today. The posting control is disabled, text is not sent to a moderator or public feed, and the visible stories are fictional examples. These terms do not promise or describe an active moderation, approval, publishing, or review-note workflow.

5. Private journals and local records

Journals, check-ins, Yoni chat history, and the other local records described in the Privacy Policy are stored in the browser on the device where Yoni is used. They are personal tools, not medical, psychological, legal, or emergency support. Do not use them as the only place to seek help during immediate danger. Avoid storing passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, or records that would create serious harm if exposed.

6. Ownership and future community participation

You remain responsible for material you write or submit. Because public Kind Wall posting is currently disabled, Yoni is not presently asking users to grant publication permission for Kind Wall content. If a real public community feature is introduced later, its submission, moderation, publication, and permission terms must be presented before that functionality is activated.

7. Prohibited use

  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, exploitation, stalking, or targeted humiliation.
  • Instructions encouraging self-harm, violence, abuse, illegal activity, or dangerous medical behavior.
  • Spam, fraud, phishing, malicious code, credential attacks, or attempts to bypass security.
  • Publishing another person’s private or identifying information without permission.
  • Presenting unverified health information as diagnosis or professional treatment.

8. Account protection and availability

Existing accounts may be restricted when reasonably necessary to protect the service, respond to suspected unauthorized access or misuse, comply with a lawful requirement, or maintain security. This section does not imply that a live Yoni community-content enforcement system is operating.

9. Availability and changes

The website may be interrupted for maintenance, security, hosting problems, or events outside reasonable control. Features may be corrected, limited, or removed when they are unsafe, inaccurate, unaffordable, or no longer useful. No promise of uninterrupted availability is made.

10. No professional relationship

Reading materials, Yoni responses, music, check-ins, journals, and any future community features are educational or supportive. They do not create a doctor-patient, therapist-client, lawyer-client, or emergency-response relationship.

11. Privacy

Current remote authentication/profile data and local browser records are described in the Privacy Policy. Registration is closed, so this agreement does not claim that the current disabled registration form is creating new legal-acceptance records.

12. Account closure and questions

Remote account access, correction, deletion, and closure requests—and the separate controls for local browser records—are explained on the Data Rights page. Questions may be sent to withlovefmb@gmail.com.
